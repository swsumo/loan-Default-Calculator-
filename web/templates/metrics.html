What does this chart mean?

This shows the mean absolute SHAP value for each feature across 500 test transactions. Higher bar = that feature has more influence on the model's fraud decisions on average. Unlike feature importance (which shows training gain), SHAP values are computed on the actual test predictions — they reflect real impact, not just what the model learned.

V14, V10, V12 are consistently the strongest fraud signals in this dataset — they represent anonymised behavioural patterns identified by the bank's PCA analysis.

Why F2-Optimised Threshold?

The default 0.5 threshold maximises balanced F1. In fraud detection, missing a fraud (false negative) costs far more than blocking a legitimate transaction (false positive).

F2 score weights recall 2× over precision. Lowering the threshold to {{ thresh.get('f2_optimal','—') }} catches 100% of fraud cases at the cost of only {{ "%.2f"|format(xgb.get('fpr',0)*100) }}% false positive rate — the right trade-off for a fraud system.
